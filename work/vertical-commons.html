Vertical Commons

Reclaiming public life above the city

Location
Taichung, Taiwan
Year
2026
  • NCKU Vertical Studio
  • Individual project
  • Instructor: Li-Wen Sung

The site, No. 11 Daguan Road in Nantun, sits on a fault line in Taichung. To the north are the glass podium towers of the 7th Redevelopment Zone; to the south, the low-rise brick street houses and temple squares of old Nantun.

The project asks how the street can rise vertically to refuse the dominance of the podium tower. A library and theatre, a sky garden and amenity floors are threaded up through the residential stack, forming a vertical street that extends the nearby forest park into the sky.
