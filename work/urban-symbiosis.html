Urban Symbiosis

Integrating ecosystems and energy networks in future cities

Location
Tainan, Taiwan
Year
2025
  • Team of five, group leader
  • Instructor: Po-Wei Lai
  • → NCKU Vertical Design Competition, First Prize

The project treats architecture as an ecosystem. An education centre integrates a double-façade system that lets buildings act as energy-generating organisms. The units are embedded in the city like chips, forming a network of energy exchange and mobility infrastructure.

More than an exhibition space, the centre is a prototype for a future urban system that joins intelligence, sustainability and technological innovation.
