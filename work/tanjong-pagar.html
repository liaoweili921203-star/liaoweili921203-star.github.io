Revitalizing Tanjong Pagar

Culture as currency in shaping a historic district

Location
Singapore
Year
2024
  • NUS exchange design studio
  • Instructor: Zdravko Trivic
  • → AYDA Awards Taiwan, Gold Award
  • → Youth Innovative Design Festival, Finalist
  • → NUS Archival, Selected Design

Tanjong Pagar presents a layered urban condition where high-rise commercial towers coexist with historic shophouses and migrant worker spaces. While the district preserves its architectural heritage, many former community gathering spaces have lost their social role as the area becomes a tourist and business destination.

The project proposes a new architectural typology that acts as a cultural catalyst. Inspired by the idea of an event city, flexible structures and spatial voids support festivals, workshops and everyday community life across three underused sites: a former church, a shophouse alley and the Yanki Playfield.
