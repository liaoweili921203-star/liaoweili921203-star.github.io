Maternity Centre in Senegal

A community healthcare infrastructure proposal

Location
Southern Senegal
Year
2024
  • Team of four: Wei-Li Liao, Ming-Ming Tan, Josh Huang, Sophia Wang
  • Role: building physical environment analysis, main architectural design

In rural Southern Senegal, high maternal and neonatal mortality stems from social factors and a lack of medical resources. The centre provides a clean space for childbirth and a hub for community health education, reviving a close-knit support system as a safe place of empowerment.

Sited on the road between two villages to serve a wider population, it isolates the delivery and operating theatres from public access through a secluded clean corridor and a scrub room.
