Downshifting

Reclaiming the space beneath the expressway

Location
Taipei, Taiwan
Year
2023
  • Team of five: Wei-Li Liao, Qiaoning Yang, Rixin Qiu, Yuting Wu, Tingjia Liang
  • Role: site analysis, residential units, main architectural design
  • → NCKU Vertical Design Competition, Selected Design

Bridges connect cities, yet beneath them lies a vast network of overlooked urban space. Downshifting transforms the space beneath elevated expressways into affordable housing, a model of collective living that slows the pace of urban life and restores social connections between residents.

Through continuous structural interventions and the idea of sharing the same roof, idle infrastructure becomes a set of communal housing platforms.
